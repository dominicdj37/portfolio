# Claims

Every public claim on the site (a number, title, employer, date, result or link) needs a row here with its source before it ships. Claims without a source, or marked "pending verification", are unpublishable. See `CLAUDE.md` and docs/workflow.md.

Status is one of: `verified` (Daniel has checked the source) or `pending verification`.

Real claims arrive in Phase 0. The placeholder home page (S11) makes none.
